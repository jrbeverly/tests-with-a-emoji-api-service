# ADR-NNNN: Short title in imperative mood

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](ADR-NNNN-short-title.md)

## Context

What problem are we solving? Describe the forces at play: technical constraints, project priorities, timeline pressure, team expertise, cost considerations. Include enough background that someone returning months later understands why this decision mattered.

## Decision

What did we decide? State the choice clearly and concisely. Include concrete details: which technology, which pattern, which approach. If the decision is "do X rather than Y", state both.

## Consequences

What happens as a result of this decision? Separate into:

**Positive:**
- What improves, what becomes easier, what risks are eliminated.

**Negative:**
- What trade-offs are accepted, what becomes harder, what new constraints apply.
